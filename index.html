Our Mission

To empower our communities with opportunities born from our collective unity and passion for foraging.

Respect

Leave no trace. Harvest ethically. Take only what's abundant and give back to the land that sustains us all.

Community

Knowledge shared grows exponentially. We build bridges between beginners and masters across every region we serve.

Reciprocity

From local parks to private lands, we steward the earth while creating business opportunities for foragers at every level.

What We Do

Business opportunities for master and beginner foragers alike — we are expanding across regions.

Free

Wild Foraging Walks

Join our free weekly guided foraging walks in local parks and preserves. Learn to identify, harvest, and prepare wild edibles — no experience needed.

  • Spring morels and ramps
  • Summer chanterelles and berries
  • Fall hen-of-the-woods and nuts
  • Winter oyster mushrooms
Paid Service

Private Land Stewardship

We partner with landowners, businesses, and chefs to assess, sustainably harvest, and manage private properties for wild edibles.

  • Property assessments & harvest plans
  • Corporate team-building retreats
  • Wild-to-table chef partnerships
  • Restoration & conservation consulting
Free

Forager Club Community

Connect with fellow foragers through Discord, skill-sharing workshops, member directories, and exclusive club events across all regions.

  • Member directory with skill badges
  • Discord community & regional channels
  • Monthly ID workshops & guest speakers
  • Supporter tier — $20 lifetime
Free

Research & Education

We document and catalog finds, create educational resources, and contribute to citizen science. Help us build a living library of local flora.

  • Specimen bank with photos & citations
  • Seasonal foraging calendars
  • Safety & identification guides
  • Community-contributed knowledge base

Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026


1. Information We Collect

We collect information you voluntarily provide when you join our club, apply for a position, or contact us: name, email address, phone number, location, and any files you upload (e.g., resumes). We also collect map pin locations you choose to share.


2. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used to: operate our forager club (communications, event coordination, team matching), process job applications, respond to inquiries, improve our services, send occasional relevant communications (with opt-out available at any time).


3. Data Sharing

We never sell your personal information. Data may be shared with: third-party service providers who help us operate (email delivery via Resend, Discord for community), team leads for coordination purposes, as required by law. Map location data is private by default and only accessible to team leads for operational purposes.


4. Data Retention

We retain your information for 12 months after your last interaction, after which it is anonymized. You may request deletion at any time.


5. Your Rights

You have the right to: access your personal data, request correction or deletion, withdraw consent at any time, export your data.


6. Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your data. However, no method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure.


7. Age

You must be 18 or older to join. If you are under 18, you need parental or guardian consent.


8. Contact

For privacy-related inquiries, contact us at privacy@quantumaggforage.com.
